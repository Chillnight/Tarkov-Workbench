# Best in category

Author: CA

The **Best in category** tab compares complete optimized builds for the moddable weapons in a selected category. A caliber filter can narrow the comparison. The regular **Weapon builder** remains available for a specific weapon and opens first. No comparison starts until **Find best weapons** is selected.

Choose **Ergo**, **Recoil** or **Balanced**, and **Silenced** or **Unsilenced**. Balanced uses an absolute Ergo target and keeps the closest achievable build when that target is unreachable. Comparisons start with iron sights and automatic magazines of any capacity; the scope and magazine pins from the regular builder are not copied into the comparison. The selected result shows its actual magazine capacity.

Trader levels, quest and barter settings, Flea Market inclusion, Arena exclusions, practical mounts, underbarrel-launcher policy, weight preference and the maximum attachment budget still apply. Weapons and included factory parts are assumed already owned, matching the regular builder. Prices and spending limits cover additional attachments only; they do not include buying the weapon or ammunition. Unknown purchase values are permitted without a spending limit and excluded from additional purchases when a limit is active.

## Ranking

- **Ergo:** higher final ergonomics first, then lower recorded vertical and horizontal recoil.
- **Recoil:** lower recorded vertical recoil first, then lower horizontal recoil and higher ergonomics.
- **Balanced:** smaller shortfall from the chosen Ergo target first, then lower recorded vertical and horizontal recoil and higher ergonomics. Builds meeting the target rank ahead of builds below it.

The selected weight/price preference resolves equal performance, followed by stable weapon names and IDs. Recoil numbers compare recorded build stats rather than simulated firing behavior. Use the caliber filter to make a more focused comparison. Ammunition, character skills and other in-game effects are outside this calculation.

Weapons that cannot produce a validated build are listed separately with their outcome. Infeasible, unproven and failed calculations are distinguished. A feasible build may be useful without having proved an optimum; results must show that limitation rather than claim a fully proved ranking. Progress counts account for checked weapons, and the comparison can be cancelled without changing a selected weapon in the regular builder.

Use **View** to inspect a result and **Open in weapon builder** to transfer its complete assembly and options to the regular builder. The selected comparison result is revalidated before transfer. Changes to settings or the database invalidate comparison results and prevent transferring a stale assembly.
