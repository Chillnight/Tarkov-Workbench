# Tactical rigs and Backpacks

Author: CA

Open **Tactical rigs** or **Backpacks** to compare items from the saved community database. Search by name, filter the results and click a column heading to sort. Select an item for its pocket layout, detailed statistics and purchase offers.

Storage capacity is the number of usable cells inside the item. The stash footprint is its external inventory size. Storage efficiency compares these two values. Separate pockets remain separate compartments, so total capacity alone does not describe which items fit.

Armoured rigs show built-in protection and removable plate slots separately. Backpack details focus on storage. Recorded base weight does not represent a loaded bag or a complete rig with every optional plate fitted.

Trader cash offers, barter ingredients and Flea Market estimates are displayed where available. Your saved trader levels and quest, barter and market settings determine which offers are marked available. Barter values and market averages are estimates. The app does not check live stock, your account progress or current listings.

Use **Update database** to retrieve current gear data and images. Downloaded data is saved in your Windows user profile for offline use. Missing gear in an older snapshot is explained with an update prompt.

Pocket layouts combine exported compartment dimensions with reviewed coordinates. Reference arrangements are documented in [layout sources](../dist/licenses/rig-layout-sources.txt). Community data and reviewed geometry can lag behind game changes.
